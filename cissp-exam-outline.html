CISSP — Exam Outline Summary

Reference · Ioannis Alexander Konstas

The Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) is ISC2's flagship certification, validating deep technical and managerial knowledge across the design, engineering, and management of an organization's overall security posture. It was the first infosec credential accredited to ANSI/ISO-IEC 17024.

Exam format

FormatComputerized Adaptive Testing (CAT)
Length3 hours
Items100–150, multiple choice and advanced item types
Passing grade700 / 1000 points
LanguagesEnglish, Chinese, German, Japanese, Spanish
Testing centerISC2 Authorized PPC and PVTC Select Pearson Testing Centers

Eligibility

Five years cumulative, full-time paid work experience in two or more of the eight domains below. A relevant bachelor's/master's degree, or another credential from ISC2's approved list, can offset up to one year of that requirement (one year maximum waived). Candidates short on experience can pass the exam and hold the Associate of ISC2 designation, with six years to complete the experience requirement.

Domain weighting

1 Security and Risk Management16%
2 Asset Security10%
3 Security Architecture and Engineering13%
4 Communication and Network Security13%
5 Identity and Access Management (IAM)13%
6 Security Assessment and Testing12%
7 Security Operations13%
8 Software Development Security10%
